## In-text forms, and three fixes to make

| Where | Currently | Should be |
|---|---|---|
| Guinea-Bissau fleet data | (Pauly et al., n.d.) | **(Pauly et al., 2020)** |
| Rejoinder / convergence | (Pauly & Zeller, 2017, 2019) | **(Pauly & Zeller, 2019)** — see below |
| FAO reply | (Ye et al., 2017) | correct as written |

**The 2017a / 2017b split matters.** There are two Pauly & Zeller papers from 2017 in this
exchange, so APA requires letter suffixes. Which one you cite where:

- The comment that started it → **2017a**
- The rejoinder to FAO → **2017b**
- The convergence → **2019 only.** The 2017b rejoinder is them holding their ground, not
  agreeing. Citing it for convergence contradicts its own title.

**First-mention forms:**

- (Basurto et al., 2024) — eight authors, so "et al." from the first citation
- (Ye et al., 2017) — seven authors, same
- (Pauly & Zeller, 2016) — two authors, always both named
- (FAO et al., 2023) — group author; you already have this right
- (Bowker & Star, 1999; Scott, 1998; D'Ignazio & Klein, 2020) — APA orders multiple
  citations in one parenthesis **alphabetically**, so: (Bowker & Star, 1999; D'Ignazio &
  Klein, 2020; Scott, 1998)

## One requirement specific to Sea Around Us

Their citation policy is not a courtesy, and it asks for two different things:

> "When referring to various datasets downloaded from the website, and/or its concept or
> design... cite its architects. Example: Pauly D., Zeller D., Palomares M.L.D. (Editors),
> 2020. Sea Around Us Concepts, Design and Data (seaaroundus.org). **When referring to a
> set of values extracted for a given country, EEZ or territory, cite the most recent catch
> reconstruction report or paper for that country.**"

You use both kinds. The 164-country tabulation is the first kind, and Pauly et al. (2020)
covers it. **The Guinea-Bissau fleet breakdown is the second kind** — strictly, they ask
for the Guinea-Bissau catch reconstruction working paper, which is linked from that
country's page on seaaroundus.org.

For an essay rather than a journal submission, citing the architects is normal practice
and nobody will object. But since Guinea-Bissau is the load-bearing example in your
strongest section, pulling that country-specific reference would be the difference between
following their policy and following most of it. It is one link from the Guinea-Bissau EEZ
page.
