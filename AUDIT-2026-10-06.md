# Full audit, 6 October 2026

Andrew asked for "a full run through of the website and make sure everything is accurate, true
and represented well". Before-copies of the page and every data file are in
`data/backups/2026-10-06-pre-audit/`.

## How it was checked

- **Live data refreshed first**, so the audit checked what a viewer sees today: El Niño and ocean
  heat (NOAA, readings of 30 Sep and 5 Oct) and the news (477 headlines, 134 countries, newest 6 Oct).
- **Four independent checkers**, each re-deriving one part from the raw source caches without using
  the build scripts' logic:
  - catch, value and fleets: about 15,800 numbers, all 282 zones and 178 country roll-ups (the page's
    own roll-up code was run in node and compared with an independent Python roll-up);
  - ecosystem: all 282 zones, national mangroves and protected waters re-fetched;
  - people, access areas, regulation, organisations, dots and conservation groups: about 4,800 fields,
    sampled links opened;
  - every sentence on the page: about 300 strings and templates.
- **Browser**: all 282 zone and 178 country panels, all six views at both scales, overlays, dot
  cards, phone width; no console errors, no broken values.
- **Links**: `check_links.py`, 0 dead.

Every headline figure held: Philippines 51% of tonnes / 62% of value / $2.4B a year; Peru 20% vs 43%;
world $137.0B, small-scale 32.3% of value vs 27.2% of tonnes; 282 zones in 178 countries and
territories. Rare's eight Fish Forever countries and Blue Ventures' eleven match their sites today.

## What was wrong, and what changed

**Said more than the data supports**
- Heat: "Share of the zone in a heatwave" was one sample area ~130 km across. Now "the sampled area";
  the note and legend say so. The coral line shows only where the zone has reefs.
- People: the jobs figure is national, inland plus marine, including processing and trade. The legend,
  tile, stat and hover now say so; territory zones say whose figure it is; 41 landlocked countries are
  no longer painted or hatched.
- "Tonnes landed" included discards: now "caught (landings and discards)".
- Country heatwave tile said "worst of 12 zones" when 4 vote: now "worst of its 4 main zones".
- El Niño: "Strong El Niño conditions" (a single seasonal index, not a declared episode); "highest
  since" is printed only while the reading is the event's own peak, otherwise "down from +X in ...".
- The small-sample warning for fish populations no longer says "very few" when the data cannot say.

**Wrong data shown**
- OBIS records far outside a species' range shown as fact (a Tasmanian handfish as a critically
  endangered chip for Finland and Poland; a polar bear, walrus, sea otter and vaquita in India's
  waters; an Atlantic sawfish in the Indian Ocean): hand lists `RANGE_ONLY` and `OUT_OF_RANGE` in
  `fetch_ecosystem.py`; the page says others may remain. Philippines: 297 → 296 species, 25 → 24 CR.
- An Amazon river turtle, a hillstream loach and a river damselfish added to `NOT_MARINE` (34 now);
  sturgeons and coelacanths filed with fish; three Pocillopora corals no longer share one name.
- "% overfished or collapsed" was the sum of two rounded shares (Cambodia 69% for 68.48%): now from the
  unrounded shares.
- Angola's law link opened a different law (2005 regulation, not the 2002 decree named): dropped.
- Five research-network (ISSF) dots had latitude and longitude reversed (a Spanish lagoon in Kenya,
  Colombian records in Antarctica); swapped back or, where unusable, placed at the country centre
  and marked approximate. 80 names lost "Address: ..." tails.
- 445 of 895 aid-project points are really a region, capital or the country itself: kept IATI's
  precision codes and drew them faint, with a card line saying so.
- Country roll-up drift: prices for single-zone countries (Falklands $4,319 → $4,243), about 40
  percentages 0.1 point off, St Helena's fleet shares. Now summed from sectors and weighted by fleet
  totals; value tonnages stored unrounded.
- Organisations: FAO was a "donor" or an "NGO" depending on which project was read first; now one
  category per body per country from all its roles. 53 placeholders ("Name Withheld", "Other
  multilateral institution") removed. Labels now "International NGOs and UN agencies" and "Other
  implementing organisations", and the note says how unrecognised bodies are filed.

**Scripts that could silently show stale or wrong data**
- `fetch_climate.py`: retries zones the server did not answer and writes nothing if more than five
  still fail or the El Niño index fails (17 zones would have shown "no reading" on 6 Oct); NOAA's
  weekly file runs negative numbers together ("20.6-0.1") and the parser skipped those weeks.
- `build_regulatory.py`: removed the stale "unstats.un.org was unreachable" sentence.

**Wording**: Applies → Boundary; "all domestic" → "none foreign"; "44 countries and territories";
"running in 2020 or later, or with no end date"; "fishery profile" in both places; confidence notes
match the legend; "All" not "Most" when one group holds every CR species; the by-value legend's hatch
also names implausible prices; Sources now lists NOAA CPC and the news sources; README corrected
(Guatemala 76% not 87%; twelve US zones not thirteen; news no longer "switched off").

## Left for Andrew to decide

- **A sovereign's own fleet counts as foreign in some of its territories.** French Guiana's 69%
  foreign is 53 points France, while Guadeloupe, Martinique and Réunion count France as domestic
  (French Guiana has its own code). Guam (USA fleet 29 points) and Hong Kong (China 44 points) now
  say so on their page.
- **"Overfished" vs "Over-exploited".** The tile and view say "Overfished"; the bar key uses Sea Around
  Us's "Over-exploited", and the method is catch-based.
- **Somalia** has a section on Blue Ventures' where-we-work page but no country tile; not added.
- **Channel Islands** are coded as part of the UK, so they show the UK's jobs rate.
- The Duke access-rights data has five enacted years that disagree with the named law's year, and
  typos ("Pilippine"); shown as published.
