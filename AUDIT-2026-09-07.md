# Overnight audit, 2026-09-07

Four auditors went over `site/index.html`, every file in `site/data/`, and every
script in `scripts/`, re-deriving every number the page shows from the raw caches
and reading every label. Then a browser run opened all 282 zone panels and all 178
country panels on every view, scale and overlay: no JavaScript errors, no
`undefined`/`NaN` in any panel. Findings were fixed where the fix was clear-cut and
left alone where it was a design decision. Everything below is reversible: the data
as it was before tonight is in `data/backups/2026-09-07-pre-audit/`.

## Fixed — wrong numbers

- **Foreign-fleet share was inflated for sparse fleets.** `fetch_fleets.py` averaged
  each fleet over the years it happened to appear in, so a fleet that fished one
  year in five was weighted as if it fished all five. Fixed to a five-year
  denominator; fleet totals now match the catch totals in 274 of 282 zones (the
  rest are zones where a sector's series ends before 2019). 33 zones moved by 2
  points or more: Northern Marianas 91 → 71%, Hawaii NW Islands 92 → 68%,
  Jarvis 75 → 52%, Syria 24 → 6%, Malta 62 → 47%, French Polynesia 46 → 33%.
  Global foreign share 17.9 → 17.4%.
- **Country page "by this country's own fleet" was 100 − foreign**, ignoring
  unattributed catch. Wallis and Futuna read 77% own fleet against a true 43%
  (34% unattributed). Now the tonnage-weighted domestic share, with the
  unattributed remainder stated, as the zone page already did.
- **Sparkline spikes.** A year in which one sector was null was charted from the
  sectors that remained: Ascension ended on "2019 · 100%" against a five-year
  share of 5%. A year is now left out when a sector that reported within the
  previous five years goes null (499 points across 61 zones). Series that have
  not started yet, or stopped long ago, keep their years.
- **A sector null through the whole window was shown as "0 t".** Now omitted
  (Jamaica's industrial series is null since 1991).
- **Northern Marianas was tagged as the USA** and wore US livelihoods and law;
  its own landmass painted "no catch data". Now `MNP`, with its own page. The
  domestic fleet alias ("North Marianas") was needed too.
- **Marine heatwave sampling point was on land for 78 zones**, so 15 large zones
  (Australia, China, India, Brazil, Chile, Madagascar…) had no reading, Iraq
  was reading Kuwait's water, and the UK Ireland's. The sample point is now
  found inside the zone, respecting the land holes in the polygon. Coverage
  266 → 275 zones; the three left are polar sea ice. Data refreshed to 6 Sep:
  ENSO is now a Strong El Niño (ONI +1.8, JJA), Peru reads Extreme.
- **Roll-up min/max let negligible zones speak for a country.** Bouvet Island's
  122 tonnes painted Norway "Poor"; a 21-tonne rock gave Chile a strong
  heatwave while the mainland had no reading. Zones under 1% of a country's
  catch no longer vote on the confidence minimum or heatwave maximum (they
  still count in every total). *This is a judgment call; revert in
  `buildCountryAgg` if you would rather keep the strict minimum.*
- **"3 ISSF" peek counted record kinds, not records.** Spain now reads
  "43 ISSF records". Case studies are counted too.
- **Organisation tally double-counted** a body named as both funder and
  implementer on one activity. "Running since 2020" excluded activities with no
  end date; now "active in 2020 or later".

## Fixed — labels and honesty

- "Fishers" tile → "Workers · employed per 1,000" (IHH counts the value chain).
- "industrial-led" peek → "largest: industrial" (the Philippines is 51%
  small-scale and its largest single sector is still industrial).
- Legend ends "100%" → "80%+", "all foreign" → "95%+ foreign", matching where
  the ramps saturate. Heatwave legend and tiles carry the reading date instead
  of "today"; country tiles say "worst of N zones".
- Confidence notes no longer claim "missing years" for zones docked only for a
  small catch.
- Territory zones now carry the same note on Preferential access and Regulatory
  that People had ("Recorded for Ecuador… may not cover these waters").
- Zones with no heat reading get an honest "no reading" section instead of
  vanishing. Hover on inland countries says "no catch data", not
  "confidence 0/4", and the cursor no longer promises a click.
- Fish Forever outline follows the scale: on the land in country mode.
- Hint text switches to "Click any country for detail". Panel scrolls to top on
  each open. PAA areas that round to 0% show "<0.01%". Law boundaries keep
  their capitals ("the 40 meter isobath", not "ofu marine park").

## Fixed — directory and pipeline

- `build_profile.py`: donor codes matched as substrings ("sida" in
  *universidad*, "ida" in *solidaridad*) filed universities as donors; EU
  Directorates-General and foreign ministries with a demonym were filed as the
  recipient's government (43 entries). Both fixed (word boundaries, demonym
  table). Annex A country strings were split on " and ", so Antigua and
  Barbuda, St Kitts and Nevis, St Vincent, Trinidad and Tobago, DR Congo,
  Alaska and Réunion organisations sat in "regional"; now placed. Secretariat
  locations no longer count as membership. ISSF names lose their appended
  postal addresses, double-escaped entities and mojibake.
- `fetch_orgs.py`: World Bank ISO crosswalk lacked Cook Islands, Niue, Saint
  Helena, North Korea; 27 activities recovered (1,422 → 1,448; 117 → 121
  countries). Script now stops rather than writing an empty file if the World
  Bank list is unreachable.
- `build_regulatory.py`: every zone's country gets a row (was World Bank
  economies only): +10 including Cook Islands, Niue, Falklands, Taiwan.
- Duplicate IATI points at one spot are drawn once.

## Left alone — design decisions for the Caleb call

- **Territory policy.** Which territories get their own code is decided by
  whether Sea Around Us happens to list the name: Falklands, Greenland, Puerto
  Rico, Guam keep their own; South Georgia, Pitcairn, Chagos, Jersey/Guernsey,
  Kerguelen, Norfolk, Heard fold into the parent, and their landmasses paint
  "no catch data" in country mode. French Guiana has a code but no landmass.
  One rule is needed; either is defensible.
- Territory zones paint the parent's People rate in zone mode (the note
  explains it, the colour does not).
- `iso_kind` says "split" for own-code territories; documented as "one
  country, several zones".
- Confidence penalises a null anywhere in 1950–2019, mostly 1950s recreational
  nulls; could be limited to the recent window.
- Sectors average present years only while fleets use a fixed denominator;
  they disagree by under 1% in 7 zones where a sector's series ends early.
- Taiwan and the Channel Islands have IHH workers but no World Bank population,
  so no per-1,000 rate.
- Kiribati's 493 subsistence fishers per 1,000 is IHH's own figure.
- README numbers drift from the files: territories are 52 not 55; ISSF flags
  199 records approximate, not 20; IATI and ISSF overlap should be recounted.

## Verified correct and untouched

Catch shares, sector splits, 70-year series and confidence scores reproduce
exactly from the raw caches for all 282 zones. People, PAA, ISSF, regulatory
and country geometry outputs reproduce byte-for-byte. All joins by zone name
and ISO3 resolve; geometry is valid with no antimeridian slivers; the paint
pipeline never sees a string or undefined. Guinea-Bissau's figures (97%
foreign; Senegal 25.5%, China 22.2%, Spain 12.8%; own fleet 33,337 t) are
unchanged.
