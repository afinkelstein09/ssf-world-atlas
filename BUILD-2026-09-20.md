## Organisation links

`data/org_links.json` was assembled by web search and then tested: a page counts only if it answered
and named the organisation on the page. Of Rare's 120 fisher organisations, 104 have a page on record
(own website, an official social page, or their profile on the WFFP or ICSF site) and 16 do not, among them
the LMMA Network, whose site refuses scripts and so could not be tested. Of the 110 most frequent
bodies in the directory, 96 have a homepage and 51 of those a page specific to their fisheries or ocean
work. Every other entry still opens to the aid activities or ISSF record it was listed from.

The link checker tells "this machine is offline" apart from "this link is dead": it tests three control
pages before, during and after a run and writes nothing if the connection dropped. The first version did
not, and marked the World Bank and UNDP dead during an outage.

## Decisions worth knowing

- **Six zones are flagged as having unbelievable prices** (a sector under $200 a tonne): Kiribati's three
  zones, Eritrea, and the industrial catch of Bahrain and Kuwait. They show their numbers under a caution and
  are hatched in the value view. The threshold is a judgment call; it sits in `fetch_value.py` as `LOW_PRICE`.
- **Species counts are never a map colour.** They track survey effort.
- **English species names come from a hand-checked table** after the automatic names from WoRMS labelled the
  whale shark "Basking shark". Species not in the table show their scientific name.
- **Stock status is catch-based.** Sea Around Us infers it from each stock's catch history against its peak.
  The panel says so beside the bar. 94 of 282 zones have no stock-status plot.
- **National figures are not inherited by territories.** Diet, GDP share, mangroves and protected waters are
  left off territory zones, and marked "national" on a zone that is one of several.
- **A search headline must belong to the country**: the headline names it (or its people, or a well-known
  coast) or the outlet is on the country's web domain. The broad fallback search for small countries requires
  the headline to name the country.
- **Google News RSS** is keyless and public but its terms are for personal, non-commercial use. One switch
  (`USE_NEWS_SEARCH`) turns it off. Worth checking before an organisation hosts the atlas publicly.
- **Thirteen FAO aid-activity titles have lost their accented letters** ("Rep blica"). That is how FAO
  published them to IATI; they are shown as published.
- **Failed downloads no longer erase data.** `fetch_value.py` and `fetch_ecosystem.py` keep the previous
  national figures if a source does not answer (found when the connection dropped mid-build).

## Known limits

- Value is in 2010 US dollars and ends in 2019, like the catch. The Pacific estimate is 2021 nominal dollars.
- The OBIS outline for the Canadian Arctic is simplified to 0.58 degrees (about 60 km); most zones are 0.02.
- Aid-activity links open IATI records on d-portal; they describe aid-funded work only.
- News coverage follows the press. The final run kept 498 headlines for 129 of 178 countries. The first,
  looser run had 865 for 172, but many of those only mentioned the country in passing; the rule that a
  headline must belong to the country costs coverage and buys relevance. English-only searching
  under-serves countries whose press writes in a language the script does not query.
- The link check on 20 September: 182 links answered, 24 refuse scripts (kept, marked unchecked), none dead.
  A random sample of 30 researcher-verified organisation pages was fetched again independently; all 30
  named the organisation.

What was learned on the way:

- **Only one free translation service worked.** Five were tested live. MyMemory translated six test headlines well (five accurate, one awkward). The unofficial Google endpoint was blocked and is outside Google's terms, no public LibreTranslate or Lingva mirror answered (one silently echoed the French back as "English"), and the offline Argos models got two of six wrong, one by reversing who accused whom. MyMemory allows 5,000 characters a day without an address and 50,000 with a contact email; the address travels in the URL and is kept by the service, so `TRANSLATE_CONTACT_EMAIL` is left empty for the owner to decide. The 42 headlines the first day's allowance did not reach were translated by Claude as a one-off, and the cache records which is which.
- **The browser's built-in translator reaches few readers.** It is stable in desktop Chrome 138+ and desktop Edge 148+ only: not phones, Safari or Firefox, and it has no Tagalog. A first use needs a click to download a language pack. In the embedded browser used for testing, the availability check never answered, so every call races a timer and failure changes nothing on the page. A skeptic agent checked the 14 key claims of this research against primary sources and confirmed all 14.
- **The atlas itself is static English.** A reader anywhere sees English labels and notes. Making the whole page multilingual is a bounded project: about 30 to 45 engineering hours for the first language and 13 to 18 hours of translation and review per language, with Spanish, Portuguese and Indonesian first. The 51 caveat sentences need a bilingual fisheries reviewer, because a drifted caveat makes the atlas say something false.
- **The news filter needed three passes.** Near-duplicate wire stories, travel pieces and celebrity items were removed after reading the results, not before.

before-copies are in `data/backups/2026-09-26-pre-review-fixes/`.

### A second review, the same afternoon

The fixes above were themselves reviewed (six reviewers, each finding challenged by a skeptic:
16 reported, 16 confirmed, 0 refuted). All sixteen are fixed:

- Two more freshwater species (a hillstream loach, a river damselfish) and the yellow-spotted
  Amazon river turtle, which OBIS holds from aquarium records and the script had filed as a sea
  turtle, joined `NOT_MARINE`; the diamondback terrapin is filed with the other reptiles. The note
  now promises only that "the freshwater and land species noticed so far have been taken out".
- Where the year's population count cannot be recovered, the page no longer says "very few": it
  says the shares fit as few as N of the n on record, or, for a year wholly in one category, that
  the shares do not say how many were classified (`n_year_max` was added for this).
- The news file records when each country's search last answered (`searched`) and which countries
  were not reached; a country whose search failed says so on its own page and keeps the date of
  the search that found its headlines, however many runs miss it. Masks gained the demonym forms
  (Bissau-Guinean, Northern Irish, New Mexican, Nuevo México).
- `langWord()` names every language, English included, so a Spanish reader's mark reads
  "translated from English", never "from en". The reef share is guarded before formatting.
- Group bars print their split in text ("Corals 140 · 2 CR · 118 EN · 20 VU") and the chip codes
  are darker; the ecosystem peek dropped the year to fit its 165 px; tile captions carry one
  caveat with the full sentence in a tooltip; the press block is moved in the DOM (not with CSS
  `order`) on laptop screens only, so tab order follows the eye and phones keep it under the card.
