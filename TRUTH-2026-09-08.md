# Truth report, 2026-09-08

Every piece of data the atlas shows, where it comes from, how it was verified, and what a
reader should still know. This is the record behind the numbers on the page as of the
files listed at the bottom.

**What "verified" means here.** Five independent checks re-derived each slice from the
raw sources with fresh code that did not import the pipeline (catch and fleets from the
Sea Around Us caches; livelihoods from the IHH workbook; access areas from the Duke
GeoPackage and CSV; regulatory from its inputs; IATI, ISSF and the directory from their
caches), over every record, never a sample. The front end was ported to Python and, separately,
the atlas's actual JavaScript was executed under Node over all 282 zone panels, 178 country
panels, 10 legends and 2,590 hover popups; the two agreed on every string. Finally a
browser run opened every panel on every view, scale and overlay: no errors, no stray
"undefined", "NaN" or "null". Findings were fixed where a fix was clear; the rest are
listed as caveats, not hidden.

**Verdict.** Every number reproduces from its source. Twelve things were wrong or
misleading on screen at the start of the pass and are fixed below. Fourteen caveats remain
that are true of the data, not defects, and the page states the ones a reader would trip
over.

## Still true, and stated on the page where it matters

- Territory policy is decided by name matching (see Catch); one rule is needed and is a decision for Rare.
- Territory zones show and paint the parent's national livelihoods figure.
- A heat reading is one 1.2° box per zone, not a zonal mean.
- IHH describes 2013–2017, and 109 of its 187 country figures are extrapolations from regional patterns rather than surveys, now flagged on the People section; Palau and Micronesia are absent.
- Confidence docks a point for any null in seventy years, mostly 1950s recreational nulls.
- Sector and fleet scripts treat a null year differently; 8 zones disagree by up to 19% on total tonnes.
- Duke's access-area polygons include estuaries; three extend beyond the atlas's zones.
- Somaliland's polygon is titled and painted as Somalia.
- Composite organisation strings are one entry; ~90 spelling variants inflate the directory.
- Country sub-lines list zones in file order, so islets can lead.
